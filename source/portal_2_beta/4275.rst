========================

.. warning::
    
    This is an old build of HL2 with unpatched vulnerabities. Only play multiplayer with people that you trust.

-------------
New Main menu
-------------

The new main menu is "diegetic": you need to click on the buttons in the level.

There is a grid of 3x2 buttons. The top-left starts a new game, the top-right quits.

On the main menu, changing some video options gets you stuck. Normally, you use Escape to exit out but that stops working.

There's also a new in-game menu.
