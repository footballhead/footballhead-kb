=======================================
Build 4107 (Feb 04 2010) (AppID 841 v0)
=======================================

Download link (repack with fixes): https://hl2-beta.ru/index.php?action=downloads;sa=view;down=366

.. note::

    The hl2-beta.ru page has incorrect paint gun information:

    -   The keys are not bound by default
    -   There are only 3 switchable paint colors bound to the left mouse button (``+attack``): bounce, speed, and stick. The cleaning paint is bound to right mouse button (``+attack2``).

    This is likely a copy-paste error from other builds.

Steam2 DB: https://femtendo.github.io/steam2-catalog/#depot-841

.. warning::

    This is an old build of HL2 with unpatched vulnerabities. Only play multiplayer with people that you trust.


---------------
Notable Changes
---------------

-   Portal 2 grabbing mechanics
-   Aesthetic closer to retail than :doc:`3916` (broken glass, lighting via projected textures)
-   Portals no longer have particle effects
-   Portals have the colored glow effect that was cut from retail
-   No Wheatley voice lines. Just on-screen text
-   The map order is dumped to the console on map load
-   Test elements that seem to have fallen out of favor: Suction, laser turrets, slowfield
-   Story being reworked from corehub to being more focused on the cores themselves
-   Get the portal gun in chamber 02 now (instead of 05)

Bugs:

-   Color correction breaks when loading a saved game

    -   Workaround: avoid using save/load system; reload the map instead

    -   Workaround: disable color correction with ``mat_colcorrection_disableentities 1``. However, this does alter the aesthetic.

    .. note::
    
        The Color Correction option in Video settings doesn't do anything

-   Water rendering glitchy (e.g. ``sp_intro_04``)
-   Alt-tabbing when full screen shows garbage for a few frames
-   One glitched frame when walking through world portals
-   The "stick" paint view controls are weird. They kinda work with the mouse and keyboard but are very broken with a gamepad.

-------------
Test Elements
-------------

Part of the single-player campaign:

-   Rocket turret (cut)
-   Security camera (cut): Scans an area of the level. Opens/closes a door when it sees the player.
-   Paint: bounce, speed, stick. Stick paint was cut.
-   Lasers
-   Light bridge (internally called "walls")
-   Aerial faith plate (internally called "catapult")
-   Turrets
-   Laserfield: kills the player on touch (likely inspired by TFV mappack)

Present but not part of the playtest:

-   Slow time (cut): player-controllable slowfield. Not actually used in any tests. Unbound by default.
-   Rotating laser turret (Cut): Uses the personality core model, attached to the ceiling by a rope. Spins 360 degrees. Attacks the player on sight. Defeated by grabbing, yanking off tether, and throwing.
-   Pneumatic diversity vent (internally called "suction") (cut): Showed off in a demo video but never released. Interestingly, only the suction part was demoed but some puzzles used a variant that blows air
