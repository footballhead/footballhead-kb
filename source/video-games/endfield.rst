Last Rite
=========

*   Cryo Striker
*   Really wants a mono-Cryo team (or at least Xaihi)

    *   Combo requires 3 Cryo Infliction. Gives Ultimate Energy based on # of stacks consumed
    *   Talent applies Cryo Susceptiblity when stacks are consumed
    *   Talent causes Ultimate to extend Cryo Susceptiblity

*   Battle skill is "delayed" - effects happen on Last Strike (it also refunds SP)
*   Game plan: stack Cryo infliction

Ardelia
=======

*   Nature Supporter
*   Battle Skill applies Physical Susceptibility and Arts Susceptibility to a single target

Gilberta
========

*   Nature Supporter
*   Battle Skill groups enemies
*   Combo Skill applies Lift
*   Ultimate grants Arts Susceptibility (buffed based on # of Vulnerability stacks)
    *   I can imagine that Vulnerability largely comes from the Combo Skill since it doesn't seem efficient to put her in a split Phys/Arts team
*   Talent gives +7% Ultiamte Gain to guards/casters/supporters

Pogranichnik
============

*   Physical (Breach) Vanguard
*   Battle Skill: applies Breach and recovers SP
*   Combo Skill (on Crush or Breach which consumes stacks): does damage and SP gain based on number of stacks
*   Ultiate: Gives 5 Steel Oath stacks. Use stacks via applying Physical status or Pog's combo skill. Does damage and restores SP.
